Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 27, 2026

Pumped Fitness ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (for email/password sign-in)
  • Display name (optional)
  • Authentication tokens from Google or Apple Sign-In

Workout Data

  • Exercise selections, sets, reps, and weights
  • Workout duration and completion dates
  • Personal records and training preferences
  • Custom routines you create

Device & Usage Data

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version and crash reports (via Sentry)
  • General usage patterns for app improvement

We do NOT collect:

  • Location data
  • Contacts or phone data
  • Photos or camera data
  • Health data (Apple Health sync is opt-in and stays on your device)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's core functionality
  • To sync your workout data across devices (Cloud Backup)
  • To generate personalized workout plans
  • To track your fitness progress and streaks
  • To send workout reminders (if enabled)
  • To diagnose and fix bugs via crash reporting

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Your workout data is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage
  • Cloud backups are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) with security rules
  • Sensitive fields are encrypted at rest
  • We use HTTPS for all data transmission
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties

4. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Authentication — for secure sign-in
  • Firebase Firestore — for cloud data backup
  • Sentry — for anonymous crash reporting (no personal data)
  • Google Sign-In / Apple Sign-In — for social authentication

5. Your Rights

  • Access your data through the app
  • Export your workout history
  • Delete your account and all associated data (Settings → Delete Account)
  • Opt out of notifications at any time
  • Disconnect Apple Health integration at any time

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all local data is immediately erased and all cloud data is permanently deleted.

7. Children's Privacy

Pumped Fitness is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@pumpedapp.net
