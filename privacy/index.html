Pumped Fitness

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Pumped Fitness is operated by Punk Labs LLC ("Punk Labs," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect information when you use the Pumped Fitness mobile application and related services (the "Service").

  1. SCOPE AND CONTROLLER

Punk Labs LLC is responsible for the personal information described in this Policy. This Policy applies to the Pumped Fitness mobile app, the Punk Labs website pages that support Pumped Fitness, account services, cloud backup, and support communications. Our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy at https://www.punklabs.dev/consumer-health supplements this Policy for consumer health data. Third-party websites and services have their own privacy practices.

  1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Account and authentication information

  • Email address, optional display name, optional profile photo, Firebase account identifier, authentication provider, and email-verification status.
  • Information returned by Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple when you choose one of those methods, subject to the permissions you grant.
  • Records needed to authenticate your account and protect it from unauthorized access.

If you choose a profile photo, Pumped Fitness requests access to your photo library only when you use the photo picker, processes the image you select, and uploads a resized copy to your private account area in Firebase Storage. Before upload, the app re-encodes the copy to remove source metadata such as embedded location information. Pumped Fitness does not upload the original image or the rest of your photo library.

Fitness and workout information

  • Exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, rate of perceived exertion (RPE), workout duration, completion dates, personal records, and exercise history.
  • Training plans, custom routines, workout schedule, rest days, streaks, muscle scores, mastery values, and other progress calculations.
  • Goals, experience level, training mode, equipment, session length, preferred workout days, and intensity preferences.

Connected Health export

  • If you explicitly connect Apple Health on iOS or Health Connect on Android, Pumped Fitness requests permission only to write completed workout sessions.
  • The exported record contains the workout name, workout type, start time, end time, duration, and an app-generated record identifier used to prevent duplicate exports.
  • Pumped Fitness does not request permission to read Apple Health or Health Connect data, including workouts written by other apps, steps, heart rate, sleep, body measurements, or medical information.
  • Your operating system stores and controls the exported record under Apple Health or Health Connect. Pumped Fitness keeps an encrypted, account-scoped local export setting, last-sync status, and workout-ID ledger to prevent duplicate exports. Those connected-health settings are not uploaded through Pumped Fitness cloud backup.

Health-related information you choose to provide

  • Selected body profile.
  • Training limitations, injury areas, or movement restrictions.
  • Hydration unit, hydration target, and daily hydration logs.

These health-related inputs are self-reported fitness preferences. Body-profile and training-limitation personalization is optional and is collected only after you make a separate in-app choice. Hydration logs are collected only when you choose to enter them. Pumped Fitness is not a medical-record system and does not verify the accuracy of this information.

Mental and personal-planning information you choose to provide

  • Goals, habits, one-time tasks, completion dates, reminder schedules, and XP reward and adjustment records.
  • Daily reflections, optional mood selections, affirmation and breathing-session completions, and quote acknowledgements.
  • Quit-habit titles, logged resets, and any historical on-track check-ins created by an earlier app version.

Mental feature information is self-directed personal content used to provide the features you request. Pumped Fitness is not a mental-health provider, therapy service, crisis service, or medical-record system. Do not enter clinical records, diagnoses, information about another person, or content you do not want stored with your account.

App preferences and device information

  • Unit, timer, notification, display, tutorial, hidden-exercise, and persistent-exercise preferences.
  • Firebase and platform providers may automatically process limited technical information such as IP address, device and operating-system details, app version, authentication events, SDK user-agent information, and app or device attestation material for security and service operation.
  • When crash reporting is configured, Sentry may process crash type, error message, stack trace, app version, operating-system and device details, diagnostic context, IP address, and the Firebase user identifier associated with the report. Pumped Fitness disables Sentry screenshots, session replay, performance tracing, failed-request capture, console breadcrumbs, and default collection of personally identifying fields.

Website information

  • Vercel processes web requests, IP address, browser and device details, approximate location derived from IP address, request timing, and security or diagnostic logs to host, secure, and deliver the Punk Labs website.
  • The website uses Vercel Web Analytics for aggregate page-view information such as page, referrer, country or city, browser, operating system, and device type. Vercel Web Analytics does not use cookies and does not associate its analytics records with an identifiable person or stored IP address. Do not place personal or health information in website URLs or query parameters.

Support information

  • If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and anything else you choose to include.

Information we do not currently collect

  • Precise device location or GPS data. Website hosting and aggregate analytics may derive an approximate country, region, or city from an IP address as described above.
  • Contacts, call logs, or text-message content.
  • Camera input, microphone recordings, or photos other than the optional profile image you select.
  • Payment-card details or purchase history.
  • Advertising identifiers or data used for cross-app tracking.
  • Apple Health or Health Connect data created by other apps or devices.
  1. OPTIONAL HEALTH PERSONALIZATION AND CONSUMER HEALTH DATA

Body-profile and training-limitation personalization is optional. Before using those entries, Pumped Fitness presents a separate notice explaining that they will adjust your body map, starting hydration goal, and exercise recommendations. You can decline and continue with neutral defaults.

When you enable this personalization, we record the choice, notice version, and time. You can withdraw the choice in Settings. Withdrawal stops that personalization and clears the saved body profile and training limitations from the active app data. It does not automatically erase workout history or hydration logs, which are separate records you created; you can remove those by clearing app data or deleting your account.

When you choose to enter a hydration log, we process the amount and date at your direction to display hydration history and progress. Do not enter medical diagnoses, clinical records, or information about another person.

Connected Health is a separate optional feature. If you choose Connect in Settings and grant the operating-system permission, Pumped Fitness writes future completed workout name, type, and timing information to Apple Health or Health Connect. It does not read data from those services and does not add invented calorie, heart-rate, step, sleep, or body-measurement values. Disabling automatic export stops future exports. On Android, Disconnect also asks Health Connect to revoke Pumped Fitness permissions. On iOS, Apple Health permissions remain under your control in the Health app. Records already exported remain controlled by Apple Health or Health Connect and must be reviewed or deleted there unless Pumped Fitness successfully removes its Android Health Connect records at your request.

We do not sell consumer health data or use it for advertising. We disclose it only to the infrastructure providers and other recipients described in Section 6 when necessary to provide a feature you request, secure the Service, comply with law, or complete a direction or consent from you.

  1. WHERE INFORMATION IS STORED

Pumped Fitness stores app data locally on your device. Sensitive local fields are protected with app-level encryption where the runtime supports it, in addition to device security controls.

When you choose cloud backup, account-linked profile, workout, hydration, plan, routine, progress, and Mental feature information is transmitted to and stored in Google Firebase. Cloud backup is optional. Firebase Authentication necessarily stores account information required for sign-in, and Firebase Storage stores the optional profile photo you choose until you replace it, remove it, or delete your account. Connected-health permission state and export de-duplication records are kept locally and are not part of Pumped Fitness cloud backup.

Local notifications are scheduled through your device. Pumped Fitness does not currently operate a server that stores notification content or push-notification tokens.

The Punk Labs website is hosted by Vercel. Website request logs and aggregate Web Analytics records are processed on Vercel systems under Vercel's applicable service terms and privacy obligations.

  1. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, verify, secure, and support your account.
  • Display and let you manage the optional profile name and photo you choose.
  • Provide workout logging, hydration tracking, Mental tools, personal planning, progress history, routines, reminders, and cloud backup or restore.
  • Generate and personalize automated training plans and recommendations from the preferences you choose.
  • When health personalization is enabled, filter or adjust exercise selections using the body profile and limitations you provide.
  • Calculate workout and personal-planning streaks, XP, personal records, muscle scores, recovery indicators, and progress views.
  • Send transactional verification, password, account-security, and support messages.
  • Deliver local notifications you enable.
  • At your direction, export completed workout name, type, and timing information to Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Prevent fraud, enforce security rules, protect Firebase resources with App Check, diagnose failures, and maintain the Service.
  • Comply with law and protect users, Punk Labs LLC, and others.

We do not use verification or security messages for advertising. We do not send marketing email unless you separately request or consent to it.

  1. HOW WE DISCLOSE INFORMATION

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and Pumped Fitness does not contain third-party advertising.

We disclose information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Google Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Storage, and App Check for authentication, optional backup, optional profile-photo storage, security, and service infrastructure.
  • Google or Apple when you select their sign-in service.
  • Apple App Attest or DeviceCheck to validate app or device integrity.
  • Apple Health or Android Health Connect when you explicitly connect the applicable service and direct Pumped Fitness to export completed workouts.
  • Sentry for crash reporting and software diagnostics when a production crash-reporting DSN is configured.
  • Expo and Apple platform services used to build, distribute, and operate the app.
  • Vercel for website hosting, security, request logs, and aggregate cookieless Web Analytics.
  • Service providers handling support communications on our behalf.
  • Courts, regulators, law enforcement, or other parties when disclosure is legally required or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • A buyer, successor, or adviser involved in a merger, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or transfer of all or part of the business, subject to applicable law.
  • Another party when you direct us or give consent.

Service providers may process information only for the services they provide and under their own applicable terms and privacy obligations.

  1. DATA RETENTION AND ACCOUNT DELETION

We retain account and cloud-backup information while your account remains active and as needed to provide the Service. Local information remains on your device until you clear it, delete your account, uninstall the app, or the operating system removes it.

You can initiate account deletion in Settings. If you cannot access the app, visit https://www.punklabs.dev/delete-account to request deletion without creating a new account. We may need to verify that you control the account before acting on a web or email request. When deletion succeeds, Pumped Fitness deletes known account-linked profile documents, workout records, routines, exercise history, hydration records, Mental feature records and events, user-owned Firebase Storage files, and the Firebase Authentication account. The app also clears locally stored account, fitness, and Mental feature data and cancels locally scheduled notifications.

If you used Sign in with Apple and initiate deletion in Settings, the app asks you to confirm your Apple identity and requests revocation of the Apple authorization as part of the deletion flow. Deletion spans Apple and Firebase systems, so Pumped Fitness reports a failure and lets you retry if every step cannot be completed. You can independently review connected apps at any time in your Apple Account settings.

Before clearing an account or local workout history on Android, Pumped Fitness makes a best-effort request to delete that account's exported Health Connect workouts by their app-generated record identifiers. If Health Connect is unavailable or permission was already removed, exported records may remain and can be reviewed or deleted in Health Connect. Apple Health records remain controlled in Apple Health and must be reviewed or deleted there. Disabling export stops future exports. Android Disconnect also requests permission revocation; Apple Health permission can be managed in the Health app.

After a deletion request begins, we retain a minimal pseudonymous deletion-control marker at the former Firebase user identifier. It contains no request timestamp, profile, photo, workout, hydration, planning, or Mental feature content. We retain this marker for as long as necessary to keep the deletion irreversible and prevent deleted consent receipts or Firestore account records from being recreated by a stale client. This security-control record is not used for advertising or analytics.

Limited security and crash-diagnostic logs, support communications, records required by law, and residual copies in service-provider backup systems may remain for the period reasonably necessary for security, debugging, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or the provider's configured retention and documented deletion cycle. Such information is not retained for advertising.

Website hosting and aggregate analytics information remains subject to Vercel's configured retention and documented service practices. Vercel Web Analytics resets the pseudonymous visitor hash used for aggregate counting every 24 hours.

If a network or provider failure prevents deletion from completing, the app reports an error and keeps the account available so you can retry or contact support.

You can:

  • Delete the account and associated cloud data through Settings.
  • Request account deletion through https://www.punklabs.dev/delete-account if you cannot access the app.
  • Contact us to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent where those rights apply.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights and the right to appeal a decision or complain to a data-protection authority. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Exercising a privacy right will not result in unlawful discrimination.

We use access controls, per-user Firebase Security Rules, App Check, encrypted network transport, device protections, and app-level local encryption where supported. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
