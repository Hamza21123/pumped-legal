Pumped Fitness Account and Data Deletion

Pumped Fitness is operated by Punk Labs LLC. You can permanently delete your Pumped Fitness account and associated data directly in the app or request help if you cannot access the app.

DELETE IN THE APP

  1. Sign in to the Pumped Fitness account you want to delete.
  2. Open Profile, then Settings.
  3. Open Data Management and choose Delete Account.
  4. Review the deletion notice, confirm the account, type the displayed confirmation phrase, and choose Permanently Delete.
  5. If prompted, confirm your Google or Apple identity. If a provider or network step fails, the app keeps the account available and tells you to retry.

REQUEST DELETION WITHOUT APP ACCESS

Email support@pumpedapp.net. If practical, write from the email address associated with the Pumped Fitness account; if you used an Apple private-relay address or cannot access that inbox, write from an address you control and include the account email. Use the subject "Pumped Fitness Account Deletion Request" and include:

  • The account email address.
  • Whether you used email/password, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple.
  • A short statement that you want the Pumped Fitness account and associated data permanently deleted.

Do not send your password, authentication code, government identifier, medical record, or other unnecessary sensitive information. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify that you control the account. You do not need to create a new account to submit a request.

WHAT DELETION COVERS

After a verified deletion succeeds, Pumped Fitness deletes known account-linked profile and settings documents, workout and exercise-history records, routines, hydration information, plans and progress records, Mental feature records and events, user-owned Firebase Storage files such as the optional profile photo, and the Firebase Authentication account. The in-app flow also clears local account data and cancels local notifications on that device.

Pumped Fitness retains a minimal pseudonymous deletion-control marker at the former Firebase user identifier to prevent stale clients from recreating deleted records. It contains no profile, photo, workout, hydration, planning, or Mental feature content. Limited security or crash logs, support communications, legally required records, and residual service-provider backup copies may remain only as described in the Privacy Policy and applicable law.

CONNECTED HEALTH

Disabling Connected Health stops future exports. Before an Android in-app deletion or app-data reset, Pumped Fitness makes a best-effort attempt to delete that account's exported Health Connect workouts while permission and Health Connect remain available. You can review or delete any remaining exported records directly in Apple Health or Health Connect. An email deletion request cannot clear app data or notifications from a device that Punk Labs LLC cannot access.
